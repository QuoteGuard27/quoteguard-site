What it does

Five jobs off your plate

The boring-but-important emails that slip when you're flat out on the tools.

  • Quote follow-up

    Up to 3 friendly emails on quotes that haven't been accepted: after 24 hours, 3 days and 7 days. Sent automatically in your approved wording, and stops as soon as the customer replies.

  • Invoice chase

    Reminders 3 days before the due date, on the due date, and at 7 days overdue. Never threatens, never adds fees, then hands it back to you. Invoice disputes come straight to you.

  • Review requests

    One polite Google review ask after every finished job, sent automatically. Same ask for everyone, no incentives, in line with Google's policy.

  • After-hours triage

    An automatic 'got your message' reply to night and weekend enquiry emails that gets the suburb and job details. Urgent jobs (burst pipe, flooding, no power, no hot water) get flagged to you straight away. The rest waits for the morning.

  • Missed-call follow-up

    When an email in your inbox has a caller's number (like a voicemail email or a forwarded note), QuoteGuard sends an email reply in your approved wording if it knows their address. If not, you get a call-back note.

  • Your wording, signed off once

    You OK the wording of each standard email at setup. Anything outside those comes to you first.

FAQ

Fair questions

Does it send emails without me?

Only the standard ones, and only in wording you've signed off. At setup you review and OK the wording of each standard email once: quote follow-ups, invoice reminders, the Google review request, the after-hours 'got your message' reply and missed-call replies (when we know the caller's email address). After that, they send automatically. Answers to real questions, anything about price or dates, complaints, invoice disputes, anything outside a template and urgent jobs always come to you first. You get a pause switch and a digest of what was sent, and you can choose an optional first week where emails are drafted but not sent.

Will it quote prices or book jobs for me?

No. QuoteGuard never quotes prices, books jobs or changes amounts. Anything about price or dates comes to you first.

Does it do SMS?

Not yet. Today QuoteGuard is email only. Phone calls and texts are coming later as an optional add-on.

Will it hassle my customers?

No. Quote follow-ups are friendly and capped at 3, and any customer reply stops the automated emails and hands the thread to you. Invoice reminders never threaten or add fees, and after the last one it hands back to you. Review requests are one polite ask per finished job. Every email has an opt-out line, and opt-outs are honoured straight away.

What do I need to get started?

A Gmail account, Google Calendar (connected read-only) and your Google review link. Then tell us your hours, what counts as urgent, your payment details and the tone you like, and sign off the wording of your standard emails.

Can I cancel?

Yes. There are no lock-in contracts. Your first month is refundable within 30 days (the setup fee is non-refundable).
